*The name and mark of ledgerbell, a paper-trading automation lab with hard risk rules.*

## Name

ledgerbell. The routines ring on the market bells (pre-market, open, midday, close), and everything the bot does is written to an append-only ledger. Two plain words joined, easy to say and spell.

Backups, in order: paperfence, bellkeeper.
